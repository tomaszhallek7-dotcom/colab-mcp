# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, diverse, and harassment-free community for all. We value the participation of every member and want to ensure that every contributor has a positive experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Publishing private information without consent
- Disruptive behavior in discussions

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards and are expected to take appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions, including but not limited to:
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and comments
- Project-related communication channels

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
